What do the zones mean?

Infected Zone: within 3 km of the infected premises. Highest risk, tightest controls.

Buffer Zone: 3–10 km. Together with the Infected Zone it forms the Control Area, where movement permits and testing apply.

Surveillance Zone: a further 10 km (10–20 km). Flocks may be tested; no movement permit.

Free Area: outside all zones.

Zones are measured from the edge of the infected premises.
